02 — About Me

Pushing the
Boundaries of
What Flies

I'm Ajinkya Jawahire, an Aerospace Engineer with over 8 years of experience across propulsion systems, aerodynamic design, and autonomous flight. My work spans high-altitude UAVs, reusable launch vehicle concepts, and next-generation satellite structures.

At Orbital Dynamics Inc., I led a cross-functional team that achieved a 23% drag reduction on our orbital re-entry vehicle using advanced CFD optimization — a result that directly improved mission payload capacity. I believe rigorous simulation and physical intuition are inseparable.

When I'm not running ANSYS Fluent simulations or building CAD assemblies, I mentor undergraduate students on capstone rocket projects and contribute to open-source aerospace tooling on GitHub.
